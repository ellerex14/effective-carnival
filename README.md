# 🍽️ Emjey Restaurant Website

### A Taste of Elegance, A Touch of Love.

🌐 **Live Website:** https://ellerex14.github.io/effective-carnival/

## 📖 About the Project

Emjey Restaurant is a beautifully designed and user-friendly restaurant website created to showcase the restaurant's menu, services, featured dishes, and overall dining experience.

The website aims to provide visitors with an elegant and welcoming online experience where they can explore delicious food selections, learn about the restaurant, and discover what makes Emjey Restaurant special.

With its modern design, attractive visuals, and organized layout, the website gives customers a glimpse of the restaurant before they even visit.

## ✨ Features

- **Home Page** – Welcomes visitors with an attractive hero section and restaurant introduction.
- **Our Story** – Introduces the restaurant and its identity.
- **Menu Section** – Showcases featured dishes with descriptions and prices.
- **Gallery** – Displays beautiful food and restaurant images.
- **Restaurant Services** – Highlights dining, takeaway, and celebration services.
- **Responsive Design** – Designed to provide a pleasant browsing experience across different screen sizes.
- **Elegant Interface** – Uses a warm, modern, and visually appealing restaurant theme.
- **Easy Navigation** – Allows visitors to explore different website sections easily.
